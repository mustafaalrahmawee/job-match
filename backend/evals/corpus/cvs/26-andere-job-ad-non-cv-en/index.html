About us

Quantaflow Technologies GmbH builds payment orchestration for mid-sized online shops: one integration, forty payment methods, smart routing between acquirers. We are 140 people from 31 countries, founded in 2019, profitable since 2024, and we process roughly 2.5 million transactions a day. Engineering is 55 people in nine product teams; English is the working language.
